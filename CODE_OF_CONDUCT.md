# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Project participants must be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Examples of
positive behavior include technical empathy, clear feedback, accepting correction,
and prioritizing user safety and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disclosure of
private information, credential sharing, or abuse of Telegram users and infrastructure
is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the hosting
platform. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. Reports will be handled
with discretion and conflicts of interest should be disclosed.
